# Power BI Dashboard Specification

Build `dashboard/customer_churn_retention_dashboard.pbix` in Power BI Desktop
using the cleaned CSVs and model reports generated by the Python pipeline.

## Data Import

Import:

- `data/cleaned/telco_churn_processed.csv`
- `reports/model_metrics.csv`
- `reports/feature_importance.csv`
- `reports/test_set_predictions.csv`

Optional:

- `data/telco_churn.sqlite`

## Model Grain

`telco_churn_processed.csv` is one row per customer.

Recommended relationships:

- `test_set_predictions[customerID]` many-to-one `telco_churn_processed[customerID]`
- Keep `model_metrics` disconnected for scorecards.
- Keep `feature_importance` disconnected for explainability visuals.

## Page 1: Executive Churn Overview

KPIs:

- Total Customers
- Churned Customers
- Churn Rate
- Monthly Recurring Revenue
- Estimated Revenue at Risk
- Average Tenure
- Average Monthly Charges

Visuals:

- Churn distribution
- Churn rate by contract type
- Churn rate by tenure group
- Revenue at risk by customer segment
- Trend-style cohort view by tenure group

Slicers:

- Contract
- Internet Service
- Payment Method
- Senior Citizen
- Tenure Group
- High-Value Customer Flag

## Page 2: Customer Risk Segmentation

Visuals:

- High-risk customers table with churn probability
- Churn probability histogram
- Risk band matrix: contract type by payment method
- Monthly charges vs tenure scatter colored by churn probability
- High-value churn risk cards

Suggested risk bands:

- Low: probability < 0.30
- Medium: 0.30 to 0.60
- High: probability >= 0.60

## Page 3: Churn Driver Analysis

Visuals:

- Top feature importance chart
- Logistic coefficient chart if Logistic Regression wins
- Churn rate by online security and tech support
- Churn rate by paperless billing and payment method
- Heatmap of numeric feature correlations

Business questions:

- Are month-to-month customers driving most churn?
- Do security or support add-ons reduce churn risk?
- Do high monthly charges correlate with churn?
- Which payment methods show the highest churn?

## Page 4: Retention Strategy Planner

Visuals:

- Segment-wise recommended retention action table
- Target population size by campaign
- Estimated revenue at risk by campaign
- Confusion matrix interpretation card
- Model recall and ROC-AUC scorecard

Example campaign segments:

- New customers with tenure <= 12 months
- High monthly charge customers on month-to-month contracts
- Senior citizens with fiber optic internet
- Customers without online security or tech support
- Customers using electronic check payment

## Formatting Guidance

- Use a restrained business palette with clear red/green risk semantics.
- Format churn and risk fields as percentages.
- Format monetary fields as currency.
- Keep customer tables dense and filterable.
- Use short chart titles that state the metric, not long explanatory text.
